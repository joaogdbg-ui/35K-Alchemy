## Families Only — immutable rules
1. Four distinctive thematic starters; no campaign may require Classic, switching families, import, advertisements, or server access to discover 100% of its listed items.
2. A+B and B+A have exactly one **global** result across all modes. Only globally registered recipes may be present in Family Only. Adding a new theme does not authorize alternative results or silent historical rewrites.
3. One item has **one** stable ID, unique name, exact artwork and identical recipes, even when listed in mythology, prehistory, fantasy, culture or science simultaneously. Genuine earned IDs synchronize among eligible campaigns; free starters are not an earned discovery, and Classic remains separate.
4. The two real rewarded-ad callbacks and persisted 1/2 state remain mandatory for optional Family-to-Classic Merge. No fake ad success in the offline HTML.
5. Each *new* family member requires a recipe path from the same four starters, and isolated closure must be tested after **every** proposed addition. Theme membership alone does not make a family completable.
