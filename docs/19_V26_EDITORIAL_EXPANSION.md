# V26 gaming expansion

- Editorial candidates are explicitly enumerated; no procedural title concatenation.
- A pair of ingredient IDs produces at most one result, order-insensitive.
- V25 catalog precedence remains immutable. Cross-franchise or noncanonical relationships do not overwrite it.
- Every newly available game has at least one reachable path from the four initial elements.
- New collectible sets use real titles. They are optional, not separate navigation.
- Game titles and platform compatibility require ongoing editorial review before commercial release.
