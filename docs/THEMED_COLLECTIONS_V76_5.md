# 35K Alchemy V76.5 — readable themed collections

All 18 Themed Collections now have a descriptive title and a short explanation visible on the card and inside its details. Examples: **Classic Platform Adventures**, **High-Speed Platformers**, **Cute Fantasy Platformers**, **Creature-Collecting RPGs**, **Anime Battle Games**, **64-Bit Game Classics**, and **Fantasy Quest Games**. Curated titles are rendered as written; generic brand replacement is not rerun over the titles.

Compatibility: the 18 `id`, `family` and `names` arrays are unchanged, as are element IDs, formulas, icons and saved progress. New `description` is display-only. Regression tests assert all titles, metadata and UI wiring.

These generic genre labels reduce confusing fictional branding but cannot certify the originality of old game titles, character art, media, audio or trademarks. Human editorial review remains necessary before commercial release.
