# V76.4 — readable generic devices + original Android launcher
Device references are now displayed as descriptive **hardware types**, such as Disc Console, Hybrid Console, Pixel Pocket, 64-Bit Cartridge Console and Arcade Console. They are not phonetic or spelling variants of established marks. Historic items and V76 SVG filenames remain stable; UI labels are rewritten through publicName/publicText, with collision disambiguation by buildPublicNameIndex.
The brand SVGs at client/assets/brand/ depict a uniquely drawn potion flask and four elemental symbols, no third-party logos, character likenesses or licensed stock images. Android CI regenerates mdpi through xxxhdpi launcher/round/adaptive PNGs and the HTML builder embeds the favicon as an offline data URI.
**Not a legal clearance**: other historic media names, music, WebP/SVG illustrations, title art and trade dress still need individual human checks and relevant INPI/EUIPO/WIPO searches before a Play Store release.
