## Art standard

- Individual subject construction; no hash-based two-icon collages, reused generic stock images, or external image dependencies.
- Muted pastel/warm palette, rounded paths, modest outlines, material-specific details, crisp silhouettes at ~35px.
- All source SVG files are uniquely SHA-256 hashed, safe for inline HTML; automated batch pHash found no pairs within <=8 Hamming distance. This is a similarity *screen*, not proof of human-level recognizability.
- Visual evidence `V69_Art_Gallery.png`, `V69_35px_Dark_Review.png`, `V69_35px_Light_Review.png` generated by `tools/audit-v69-art.py`.
